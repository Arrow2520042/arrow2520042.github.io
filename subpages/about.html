    Vintage Technology & Collections

    Privately, I am a man of many interests. I passionately cultivate tropical plants, which require patience and care, and for years I have also enjoyed collecting Polish coins. I am the creator and author of tapesandcassettes.com. I built it out of a desire to rescue vintage technology from oblivion. There, I proudly showcase my diverse collections of data storage media, audio and video tapes, and unique computer processors. Beyond the hardware itself, I also collect fascinating, often untold stories from the worlds of IT, Audio, and Video, documenting the people and technologies that once changed the course of history.

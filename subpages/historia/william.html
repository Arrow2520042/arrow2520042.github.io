    The Story of How William from China Helped Me Secure the Rare CBHD and EVD Discs

    My journey with the CBHD format (a high-definition disc format developed in China, now a true technological rarity) began over six years ago. For years, I searched high and low for a disc to add to my collection, but without success—until one day, while browsing Chinese websites, I stumbled upon a listing in a local bookstore. It felt like pure luck, especially since the price was incredibly low! However, my excitement was quickly met with a challenge. I hit a wall during checkout: no PayPal, no international credit cards... only Chinese banks or the "Weixin Pay" app, which repeatedly rejected my identity verification. As days passed, the seller grew impatient and threatened to cancel the order. In a last-ditch effort, I thought of our company’s Chinese supplier. With the help of a colleague who connected me with the Polish headquarters, I was introduced via email to William, who is responsible for sales in Poland. Without a moment's hesitation, William offered to help. I sent him the link, and moments later, the item vanished from the store—William had secured it. He simply told me to wait patiently. A month later, I received a call. It turned out that William had organized a brilliant logistical operation: he bought the discs, had them shipped to his mother in China, and she, in turn, passed the package to a colleague who was flying to Poland. When William called, he said, "Hey, I'll drop by tomorrow with your discs." When I told him that might be difficult since he was in Warsaw and I was in Krakow, he simply replied, "No problem, I’ll be at your place in two days! "True to his word, he arrived. We finally met in person, exchanged gifts, and had a wonderful, productive conversation. Thanks to William’s immense kindness and goodwill, I finally added those elusive CBHD discs to my collection, along with a rare EVD disc as well. Today, these discs are the pride of my collection and the envy of my fellow collectors! To this day, I have never encountered these formats again.

    William, thank you once again for your incredible help in building my collection!
